Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

In short

  • Your first month is free — cancel during the trial and you pay nothing.
  • After that it's ₹4,999 per month, and you can cancel any time.
  • Cancel before your next billing date and you won't be charged again.
  • Months already paid for are not refunded.

1. Free trial

New customers get their first month of LocalPulse free. No payment is taken during the trial. If you cancel at any time during the trial, you will not be charged anything.

2. Cancelling a paid subscription

After the free trial, LocalPulse is billed at ₹4,999 per month, in advance. You can cancel at any time. To avoid being charged for the next month, cancel before your next billing date. After you cancel, your service continues until the end of the month you have already paid for, and then stops.

3. Refunds

Because you can try the service free for a full month before paying, payments for a month that has started are not refundable, including for partially used months.

The only exception is a payment made by mistake on our side, such as a duplicate charge or a charge after you had cancelled on time. These are refunded in full to the original payment method, normally within 7 working days of being confirmed. Your bank may take a few extra days to show the refund.

4. How to cancel

Send us a WhatsApp message or call us with your business name and the phone number you registered with. We will confirm your cancellation within 2 working days.

WhatsApp or call us on +91 78922 18476 (open WhatsApp).

5. After you cancel

  • We stop creating and publishing posts once your paid period ends.
  • Posts already published stay on your Google Business Profile. You can keep them or delete them.
  • You can remove our manager access from your Google Business Profile at any time.
  • We delete your business details and brand material within 30 days, as described in our Privacy Policy.
