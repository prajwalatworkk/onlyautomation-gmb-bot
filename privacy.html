Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Hub 360, a partnership firm based in Bengaluru, India ("we", "us"), collects, uses and protects information when you visit justautomate.si or use our services, including LocalPulse. It is written to meet our obligations under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information we collect

When you contact us or sign up

  • Your name, phone number and WhatsApp messages you send us.
  • Your business details: business name, website, industry, products and services, address and target keywords.
  • Brand material you share with us, such as your logo and brand colours.
  • Your posting preferences: days, time, topics and settings.
  • Billing details needed to issue invoices.

When we run LocalPulse for you

  • Manager access to your Google Business Profile. You add our team as a manager from your own Google account. We never ask for, receive or store your Google password.
  • Public content from your website, which we read to understand your business.
  • The posts and images we create for you, and a record of when each post was published.

When you visit our website

  • Our website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track you across other websites.
  • Our hosting provider automatically records standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to keep the website secure and running.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers.

2. How we use your information

  • To set up and deliver LocalPulse: writing posts, creating branded images and publishing them on your Google Business Profile on your schedule.
  • To communicate with you about your service, including support, updates and billing.
  • To issue invoices and keep records required by law.
  • To improve our services and keep them secure.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Service providers we use

We share information only with providers that help us deliver the service, and only as much as they need:

  • Google — Google Business Profile (where your posts are published) and Google's Gemini AI, which helps write post text from your business details and website content.
  • Vercel and GitHub — hosting our website and storing service settings.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — for messages you exchange with us.
  • Payment partners — if you pay online, your payment is processed by our payment partner. We do not store your card or bank account details.

Some of these providers may store data on servers outside India. We may also disclose information if required by law or by a valid request from a government authority.

4. How long we keep information

We keep your information for as long as your service is active. After you cancel, we delete your business details, brand material and settings within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as invoices). Posts already published on your Google Business Profile stay on your profile, which you control, until you delete them.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including using access you grant through Google's own manager permissions instead of passwords, and limiting access to the people who run your service. No method of storing or sending data online is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will inform you if a breach affects your data.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you and your business.
  • Correct or update incorrect information.
  • Delete your information — see our Data Deletion page.
  • Stop using your information, by cancelling your service.

You can remove our manager access from your Google Business Profile at any time without asking us.

7. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make important changes, we will let active customers know.

9. Contact and grievances

For privacy questions, requests or complaints, contact us. We aim to respond within 7 working days.
