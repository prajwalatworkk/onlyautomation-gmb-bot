About us

Built for businesses that can't afford to stop.

onlyautomation.in is a suite of automation tools for Indian small and medium businesses — helping them stay visible online without spending hours on social media.

Our story

Marketing that keeps running, even on your busiest days.

Why we built this

Running a small business in India means wearing every hat — sales, operations, customer service, and somehow also digital marketing. Most owners know they should post regularly on Google, Instagram and WhatsApp, but they never have the time to actually do it.

We built onlyautomation.in to solve exactly that. Set your schedule once, and our tools handle the rest — the writing, the design and the posting. You focus on your business.

Who it's for

Small and medium businesses across India — clinics, restaurants, retailers, local services, consultants and agencies — anyone who needs a consistent online presence but doesn't have a full marketing team. If you have a Google Business Profile and want more people to find you, LocalPulse is for you.

Who built it

onlyautomation.in is built and run by Digital Hub 360, a digital marketing agency in Bangalore. We manage Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO and lead generation for Indian businesses — and we built these tools to make our clients' lives easier. Now we're making them available to everyone.

What we stand for

Simple tools. Honest results.

Made for India

Built for Indian businesses — local context, local customers and the platforms they actually use.

You stay in control

No passwords are shared. You add us as a manager on your profile and can remove access whenever you like.

Zero manual work

Once it's set up, every tool runs on autopilot. No daily logins and no manual posting.

No false promises

We won't promise you the #1 spot on Google. We promise a profile that stays active, every single week.
