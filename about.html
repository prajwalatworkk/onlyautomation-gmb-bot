About Us
Built for businesses that can't afford to stop.

onlyautomation.in is a suite of automation tools built for Indian small and medium businesses — helping them stay visible online without spending hours on social media.

Why we built this

Running a small business in India means wearing every hat — sales, operations, customer service, and somehow also digital marketing. Most SMB owners know they should be posting regularly on Google, Instagram, and WhatsApp — but they never have time to actually do it.

We built onlyautomation.in to solve exactly that. Set your schedule once, and the bots handle everything — AI content, branded images, posting, logging. You just focus on your business.

Who it's for

Small and medium businesses across India — local services, agencies, consultants, retail shops — anyone who needs a consistent online presence but doesn't have a full marketing team. If you have a Google Business Profile and want people to find you, LocalPulse is for you.

Who built it

onlyautomation.in is built and maintained by Digital Hub 360, a digital marketing agency based in Bangalore. We manage Google Ads, Meta Ads, SEO, and lead generation for Indian businesses — and we built these tools to make our clients' lives easier. Now we're making them available to everyone.
