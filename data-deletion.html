Data Deletion

Last updated: 2 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the information we hold about you and your business at any time. You can also remove our access to your Google Business Profile yourself, without contacting us.

1. Remove our access to your Google Business Profile

  1. Open your Business Profile on Google (search for your business name while signed in, or go to business.google.com).
  2. Open the menu and choose Business Profile settings, then Managers (sometimes called People and access).
  3. Select our account and choose Remove.

Once removed, we can no longer publish or change anything on your profile.

2. Ask us to delete your data

Send us a WhatsApp message or call us with your business name and the phone number you registered with, and say you want your data deleted.

WhatsApp or call us on +91 78922 18476 (open WhatsApp).

We will confirm your request within 2 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

3. What we delete

  • Your business details, keywords, notes and posting settings.
  • Your logo and brand colours.
  • Copies of posts and images we created for you, and our records of when they were published.

4. What we may keep

  • Invoices and payment records that we are required to keep by law.
  • Posts already published on your Google Business Profile. These are on your profile, which you control — you can delete them from Google at any time.

Deleting your data also ends your LocalPulse service. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle data.
