Service Delivery Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

LocalPulse is a digital service. Nothing is shipped physically — the service is delivered online, as posts published on your Google Business Profile.

1. Getting started

  1. Contact us on WhatsApp or by phone to start your free trial.
  2. Share your business details — website, industry, products and services, the keywords you want to rank for, your logo and your preferred posting days and time.
  3. Give us manager access to your Google Business Profile from your Google account. We will guide you through it.
  4. We set everything up, usually within 2 working days of receiving your details and access.

2. When posts go live

  • Your first post is published on your next scheduled posting day after setup.
  • After that, one post is published on each of your scheduled days, at your chosen time (Indian Standard Time).
  • Google reviews new posts before showing them publicly. This usually takes minutes but can sometimes take longer, and is outside our control.
  • If a post cannot be published at the scheduled time, our system retries the same day.

3. Changes to your service

You can ask us to change your posting days, time, topics, keywords or branding at any time. Changes are usually applied within 1 working day.

4. Service area

LocalPulse is offered to businesses in India with a Google Business Profile.

5. Problems with delivery

If you notice that posts are not appearing as expected, contact us and we will look into it.
