FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost? Is there a free trial?

LocalPulse is ₹4,999 per month, and your first month is free. We set everything up, you see the posts going live on your profile, and you decide whether to continue.

Do I need to share my Google password?

No. You add our team as a manager on your Google Business Profile. You remain the owner and can remove our access at any time from your profile settings.

How often will you post?

On the days and at the time you choose — one post on each scheduled day. You can change the schedule whenever you want.

What will the posts be about?

Useful updates and tips about your products and services, written from your website and industry, using the keywords you want to be found for. Topics rotate so your posts don't repeat.

Will the images have my branding?

Yes. Each image uses your logo and colours picked from your logo, along with a headline and key points from that day's post.

Can I check posts before they go live?

Right now posts go out automatically on your schedule. Approving each post on WhatsApp before it goes live is coming soon.

Will this guarantee I rank first on Google?

Nobody can honestly guarantee rankings. Regular, relevant posts keep your profile active and give customers more reasons to call — which is what LocalPulse takes care of for you.
